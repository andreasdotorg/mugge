## US-166: USBStreamer Residual ERR Audibility Assessment

**As** the audio engineer/operator,
**I want** to assess whether the residual USBStreamer ERR (~1 ERR/min after
period-num=5 fix) are audible during DJ and live playback,
**so that** I can decide whether further mitigation is needed before the gig.

**Status:** selected
**Priority:** Medium (should-have)
**Effort:** ~0.5 worker days (measurement + listening test)
**Origin:** Session 16 gig sprint — post F-295/F-296 residual assessment
**Depends on:** US-161 (FIFO/70 deployed and verified)
**Blocks:** none
**Cross-references:** F-295 (root cause: USB jitter + tight buffer margin),
change-S-007 (F-295 lab note), D-066 (FIFO/70 for JACK clients)

### Background

After the F-295 fix (period-num 4->5) and the F-296 fix (FIFO/70 for Mixxx),
the ERR rate dropped from ~16/min to ~1/min in the core graph. This residual
~1 ERR/min is caused by USB isochronous transfer jitter on the VL805 xHCI
controller — a hardware constraint that cannot be eliminated in software.

The question is: are these remaining ERR audible? An ERR in `pw-top` means a
missed graph cycle deadline. Whether it produces an audible artifact depends
on the buffer depth and the nature of the missed cycle.

### Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC #1: **Baseline ERR measurement.** With FIFO/70 deployed and all
      production nodes running (including metering), measure ERR rate over
      a 30-minute DJ session. Document per-node ERR rates.
- [ ] AC #2: **Listening test.** Owner listens to full signal chain (through
      headphones or monitors at normal listening level) during a 15+ minute
      DJ session. Assessment: (a) no audible artifacts, (b) occasional but
      acceptable artifacts, or (c) unacceptable artifacts.
- [ ] AC #3: **Live mode ERR measurement.** Same measurement at quantum 256
      (live mode). Document whether tighter quantum increases ERR rate.
- [ ] AC #4: **Mitigation options documented.** If ERR is audible and
      unacceptable, document options: period-num=6, q2048 fallback, PW 1.6.2
      JACK bridge investigation, or USB audio interface change.

### Definition of Done

- [ ] All acceptance criteria met
- [ ] AE consultation: interpret ERR rate vs audibility relationship
- [ ] Lab note with ERR data, listening test results, and recommendation
